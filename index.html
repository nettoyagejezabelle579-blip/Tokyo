About the data

Yamanote Line weekday departures are the JR East Shibuya table (2026). Weekend/holiday Yamanote rows and some Ginza Line hours are filled from the published headway for that hour. Scramble signal cycle: 120 s, pedestrians first. Buildings are simplified but placed where they stand: Shibuya Scramble Square 229.7 m, Hikarie 182.5 m, Stream 180 m, Sakura Stage 179 m, Fukuras 103 m. Mt Fuji, Tokyo Tower, Skytree and Shinjuku sit at their real bearings.
